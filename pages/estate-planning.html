Estate planning

Make sure your bitcoin reaches your family.

Self-custodied bitcoin can be lost forever if your heirs don’t know it exists or how to access it. A little planning now can save your family a lot of heartache later.

Book a free 15-minute intro call

🔒 Our promise

We never see, record, or hold your seed phrase or private keys. You do the private parts yourself.

We work alongside your lawyer or notary; we don’t draft wills.

The problem

A bank account has a branch, a statement, and a process for executors. Bitcoin in a hardware wallet has none of that. If no one knows where the wallet is, which backups matter, or how to use them, the bitcoin may simply be gone — and no company can recover it.

Even bitcoin on an exchange or held as an ETF in a TFSA or RRSP can be slow and stressful to find if it isn’t written down.

What we help with

📋

Inventory of holdings

A clear list of where your bitcoin lives: exchanges, ETFs in a TFSA or RRSP, and hardware wallets. No keys or passwords go on our copy.

🗺️

An access plan

Who needs to know what, where things are kept, and how an heir would actually get access — step by step.

✉️

Letter of instructions

A plain-language template for your executor. You fill in the private details yourself and store it with your estate documents.
