Books that age well

Buy paper if you can. You will highlight it. Titles and availability change — search your local shop or library.

Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System

Satoshi Nakamoto · 2008 · white paper

Nine pages. The source document. Read it once slowly, then again after you hold some sats.

The Bitcoin Standard

Saifedean Ammous

Why hard money matters, in economic history terms. Opinionated. Useful even if you argue with parts of it.

The Price of Tomorrow

Jeff Booth · Canadian author

Technology, deflation, and why an inflationary unit of account fights the future. A strong Canadian entry point.

Broken Money

Lyn Alden

How money actually moves: ledgers, credit, and why Bitcoin is a new settlement layer. Dense, fair, current.

Inventing Bitcoin

Yan Pritzker

The shortest technical explainer that still respects the reader. Proof-of-work without the fog.

The Blocksize War

Jonathan Bier

How Bitcoin’s governance actually worked under pressure. Required if you want to understand “who controls this.”

Layered Money

Nik Bhatia

Gold, treasuries, eurodollars, and Bitcoin as a new base layer. Short and structural.

Check Your Financial Privilege

Alex Gladstein

Human rights, capital controls, and why “number go up” is not the whole story.

Canadian tools & people

Independent educators and platforms. Vet them yourself. We are not them.

BTC Sessions

Ben Perrin’s tutorials for wallets, Lightning, and self-custody. Watch before you click.

Bull Bitcoin

Non-custodial Canadian exchange. A different model than an app that holds bitcoin for you.

Shakepay

Beginner on-ramp with Interac. Referral link (disclosed) or go direct.

Learning Bitcoin

Workshops and conferences (Victoria / Vancouver area). Hands-on, not a pitch deck.

Bitcoin Coalition of Canada

Policy and education directory. Useful map of other Canadian educators.

BTCPay Server

Open-source invoicing for businesses that want to control their payment stack.

What we will not list

Leverage products, “yield” on bitcoin, altcoin casinos, recovery services that ask for your seed, and anyone who DMs you first. If a resource cannot survive a boring Tuesday, it does not belong here.
