How to get off zero

Five calm steps. Start small. Keep records. Keep learning.

Learn the basics

What Bitcoin is, why 21 million matters, and the difference between holding bitcoin and trading noise.

Set up a wallet

Start with a reputable app. When the amount matters to you, move to a hardware wallet you control.

Buy a small amount

For most Canadians, Shakepay is a simple first step with Interac e-Transfer. Treat the first buy as tuition.

Hold with a plan

Write down your seed phrase on paper. Never share it. Enable 2FA. Ignore daily price theatre.

Know the Canadian tax basics

Buying and holding is generally not a taxable event. Selling, swapping, or spending usually is. Keep records.
